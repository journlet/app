### Summary

The requirements are well structured, use stable IDs, trace most decisions to confirmed answers, and match the code on current behaviour (6 s toast, "nothing logged" text, sticky capture state). They are not yet implementable without guessing: one real contradiction on where a task lands (R-01), two unconfirmed [assumption] items that decide behaviour (R-01, R-02), an undefined "More" interaction with the bar's remembered state (R-03), and several untestable thresholds (R-04 to R-06). I expect R-01 to R-06 to need a short follow-up question round.
