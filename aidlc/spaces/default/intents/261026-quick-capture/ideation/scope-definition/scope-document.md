# Scope Document: Quick Task Entry

## Goal

With the app already open, a person adds a task with one tap, then typing, then Enter, and it is saved within about 5 seconds (intent statement, Success Metrics).

## In Scope

- Comparing four quick-entry options as rough mockups before one is chosen [Q1]:
  - **Add in place:** tap a section (for example today) and a line opens right there with the keyboard up.
  - **Slimmer bottom bar:** tapping "Log an entry…" opens the keyboard on a small field instead of the full-screen form.
  - **Always-ready line:** an entry line at the end of each day or week section that you tap and type into.
  - **Faster full form:** the full-screen form opens ready to type, with the page and type already filled in from where you are.
- Pressing Enter saves the task and closes the entry, returning to the page [Q2].
- A button beside the entry line opens the full form with the typed text already in it, for anything beyond a plain task [Q3].
- A quick entry is always a task [Q4].
- Quick entry on the day, week, month and year pages and on your own collection pages, all working the same way [Q5].
- Deciding, once the mockups are compared, whether the current "Log an entry…" bar stays alongside the new entry or is replaced [Q6].

## Out of Scope

- Getting into the app faster, such as home-screen shortcuts: the confirmed boundary starts with the app already open (intent statement, Initial Scope Signal).
- Choosing a different entry type, priority or other details during quick entry; these stay in the full form, reached through the edit-further button [Q3] [Q4].

## Minimum Viable Scope

Quick entry on the day page, using the option chosen after the mockups, with Enter to save and the edit-further button [Q2] [Q3] [Q7]. The day page comes first in the agreed order [Q7].

## Sequencing

- Day page first, then week page [Q7].
- Month, year and collection pages are in scope now [Q5]; their order after the week page has not been set.
- No deadline [Q8].

| Step | Today | After this work |

## Assumptions & Open Questions

- The speed target needs fixed start and stop points, a definition of a "short" task, and a measurement of today's taps and time as a baseline. Carried from the intent review for requirements analysis to settle. [assumption]
- The order of the month, year and collection pages after the week page is not yet set. [assumption]
