### Summary

The requirements are well traced to the intent and the confirmed answers. They use Given/When/Then, the main flows are covered, and scope boundaries are explicit. Three Major gaps could cause rework: the done/struck rule on the entry path, the unannounced time-of-day reduction, and the "following the series" edge cases. Each has a clear workaround, so engineering can start, but I recommend settling these before approval or at the next design step.
